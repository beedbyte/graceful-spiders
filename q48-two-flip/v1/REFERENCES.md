# Prior constructions and priority boundary

The passages cited below document earlier operations on graceful path permutations: block concatenation, endpoint-compatible path gluing and bipartite permutation insertion. Attribution in this list is limited to those path operations. The exact q48 positional certificate is documented without claiming a new primitive path operation. Worldwide priority for the exact all-parameter positional consequence is UNKNOWN; absence from a bounded search is not evidence of novelty.

1. Jacob Hicks, M. A. Ollis and John R. Schmitt, *Distinct Partial Sums in Cyclic Groups: Polynomial Method and Constructive Approaches*. [Author-hosted manuscript](https://community.middlebury.edu/~jschmitt/papers/HicksOllisSchmitt2018.pdf), dated29 January2019, proof of Lemma4.5, PDF page14. The proof concatenates shifted graceful and even bipartite graceful blocks with a matching endpoint. The URL filename contains2018; the accessible manuscript itself is dated2019.
2. Michał Adamaszek, *Efficient Enumeration of Graceful Permutations* (2006). [Original arXiv manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0608513), Lemma1 and its proof, PDF page4. It describes endpoint-compatible gluing with separate shifts of bipartite label classes, crediting earlier references. This is an antecedent operation, not a proof of the simultaneous midpoint/extreme conditions required by the present literal certificate.
3. M. A. Ollis, Australasian Journal of Combinatorics78(1) (2020),35–60. [Journal original](https://ajc.maths.uq.edu.au/pdf/78/ajc_v78_p035.pdf), Section5, Lemma5.5 and Theorem5.6, printed page53/PDF page19. These concern bipartite graceful permutations and insertion. Lemma5.5 cites[21], A. Kotzig (1973); it must not be attributed to Gvozdjak[17].

These passages establish older path-permutation operations. The graph-level transfer from a midpoint-alpha path and a root-zero residual to the actual spider is proved directly in INVENTORY.md and the Lean sources. This list supplies no pinpoint historical attribution for that graph-level transfer, and makes no claim that the transfer is new. This package does not prove that the exact two-flip family is, or is not, a direct consequence of every earlier construction. The original Cattell *Graceful labellings of paths* (2007) full-text comparison remains incomplete in the project's literature record. Separate one-point freedom and simultaneous midpoint/extreme constraints must not be identified without proof. No worldwide novelty or external-review claim follows from the formal verification.
