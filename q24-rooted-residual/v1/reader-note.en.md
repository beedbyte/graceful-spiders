# Prescribed zeros on two arms of a rooted graceful graft

For each `t≥1`, set `K=23+24t`, `d₀=20+22t`, and `d₁=21+22t`. Start with any conventionally graceful indexed graph `H` with `Q` edges: distinct vertex labels lie in `0,…,Q`, and its edge differences are exactly `1,…,Q`. Select a root whose label is zero, then attach two named paths of `K` edges at that root, retaining all of `H`. Each arm separately admits a conventional graceful labeling with zero at depth `d₀` and another with zero at depth `d₁`. Four separate labelings may be used. No tree, alpha, connectivity, or vertex-onto assumption is made for `H`; no old residual vertex, tip, or other depth is covered.

The high-side shift and alpha/graceful amalgamation overlap the established operation described by Christian Barrientos, “On the generation of alpha graphs” (2022). The checked passage does not establish the four specified interior positions. Worldwide priority is unknown.
