The general alpha-vertex amalgamation operation is due to Huang, Kotzig and Rosa. Panpa, Imnang and Wasuanankul (2025, Theorem 2.5 and proof) restate it: identify an endpoint-labeled vertex of an alpha-labeled graph with the zero-labeled vertex of a graceful graph, using reverse alpha-labeling where needed. Shan and Zhong (2026, Lemma 1 and its explanation) also state the operation and describe its label shifts. These sources directly credit the generic rooted graceful-graph/alpha-graph graft used by the construction; they do not state the exact arbitrary-H, every-position K95 result here.

- Panpa, A.; Imnang, S.; Wasuanankul, T. “Graceful Labeling of Spider Graphs With at Most Five Legs.” *Journal of Applied Mathematics* (2025), article 5826777. [Publisher article](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1155/jama/5826777).
- Shan, S.; Zhong, Y. “Graceful Labeling of Two Families of Spiders.” 2026. [Author preprint, version 2, Lemma 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.14295v2); published version DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00373-026-03064-2.
- Barrientos, C. “Alpha graphs with different pendent paths.” *Electronic Journal of Graph Theory and Applications* 8(2) (2020), 301–317, DOI 10.5614/ejgta.2020.8.2.8. [Publisher PDF](https://www.ejgta.org/index.php/ejgta/article/download/1036/pdf_143). Printed pp. 302–303 describe the high-side d-graceful shift, translations, complement and a narrower alpha-graph amalgamation with two alpha inputs. This is operation overlap, not a verbatim theorem for arbitrary nonbipartite H.

The present K95 theorem uses established operations to build the stated arbitrary-H result. No new primitive operation is claimed; exact positional-family priority remains **UNKNOWN**. This bounded comparison does not assert exhaustive subsumption or external review.
