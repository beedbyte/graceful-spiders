# Sources and coverage for the K7 note

Prepared 9 October 2026 from the literature review dated 8 October 2026. This is a local source map; no originality claim is made. Primary results are distinguished from deductions made in the review.

| Source | Relevant statement | Coverage and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brandon J. Patterson, *New Classes of Graceful Spiders and Related Computational Results*, master's thesis, Ball State University, May 2017. [University PDF](https://cardinalscholar.bsu.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/b44ff232-a480-4d64-aa44-8dff4535e28c/content) | Theorem 3.3.8, printed pp. 13–14 (PDF 14–15); Corollary 2.4.3, p. 7; Theorem 3.1.3, p. 10; Theorem 5.3.6, p. 26; Conjecture 5.4.4, p. 27 | The entire K7 family is already α-graceful. The broader zero-rotatability statement is a conjecture. Removing a long arm, labeling the remaining center zero, and reversing the leaf reduction yields tip zero; complementing yields depth 6. These family applications are deductions from the thesis. The finite census in Appendix A stops at order 16, while K7 starts at order 23. |
| A. Panpa, S. Imnang, T. Wasuanankul, *Graceful Labeling of Spider Graphs With at Most Five Legs*, Journal of Applied Mathematics (2025), DOI 10.1155/jama/5826777. [Original full text](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1155/jama/5826777) | Theorems 3.2, 3.3, 3.4 | Center zero for three legs; any prescribed leaf zero for four legs; gracefulness for five legs. This directly addresses leaves of K7 at m=1 and gracefulness at m=2. It does not state arbitrary inner zero positions for every m. |
| Songling Shan, Yucheng Zhong, *Graceful Labeling of Two Families of Spiders*, arXiv:2605.14295v2 (2026). [Versioned record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14295v2) | Theorem 5, printed p. 3; contrast Theorems 2 and 4 | All but three legs may have length at most 2: every K7 case is graceful. Theorem 4's center-zero family has only one unrestricted leg, so it does not directly apply here. These statements do not supply the full prescribed-zero result. |
| Rafael I. Rofa, *0-rotatability of classes of rooted symmetric trees. Are rooted symmetric trees 0-rotatable?*, arXiv:2312.16235 (2023). [Original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.16235) | Theorem 2 and Corollaries 1–3 | Root symmetry and an additional caterpillar remainder condition matter. Uniform spider legs of length at most 3 have a full rotatability result; mixed lengths 7 and 1 do not satisfy that stated class. |
| Patrick Bahls, Sara Lake, Andrew Wertheim, *Gracefulness of families of spiders*, Involve 3 (2010). [Publisher PDF](https://msp.org/involve/2010/3-3/involve-v3-n3-p01-p.pdf) | Theorem 1 | Near-equal lengths r and r+1, with center zero in the odd-leg construction. Uniform S(7,7,7) supplies a center-zero base; maximal center-leaf additions imply gracefulness of the present family. The authors trace that result to Poljak–Sura (1982). No arbitrary-zero assertion follows directly. |
| Bogdan Dumitru, Mihai Nacu, *Alternating Extremes in Graceful Labelings of Full Binary Trees and Spider Trees*, arXiv:2607.12597 (2026). [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12597) | Theorem 3.3 and Corollary 3.6 | Fixed nontrivial leg lengths with sufficiently many center leaves; center label 1 and a zero in an arm. Does not provide every m≥1 or every prescribed zero vertex. |
| Tong Niu, *Graceful labelings of spiders with three-edge legs and pendant leaves at the center*, arXiv:2605.02303 (2026). [Record with withdrawal note](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02303) | Abstract and withdrawal note as recorded in the local literature review | Concerns gracefulness with three-edge arms. Withdrawn on novelty grounds. It is context for careful attribution of leaf extension; it is not used to prove K7. |

The thesis is a master's thesis, not a doctoral dissertation. The title-page author is **Brandon J. Patterson**. PDF page numbers are one greater than printed page numbers. Its full local 104-page PDF and extracted text were available to this preparation; the former access gap in the public V1 copies is closed. The construction results and Chapter 5 were examined in full, and the appendices were checked for their scope and tabulation purpose. This preparation does not claim an independent recalculation of the approximately 70 million finite labels.

The archive excludes third-party papers. Their local SHA-256 values may identify the versions inspected without granting redistribution rights. Links identify the originals; inclusion of PDFs would require a separate rights decision.

The historical review is targeted rather than an exhaustive backward and forward citation search. The complete all-m prescribed-zero statement was not located in the explicitly examined primary statements, but other literature or general composition theorems may cover it. No first-proof, new-method, or worldwide novelty claim is made.
