## Exact target and the decisive implication question

The potential statement is: for **each** natural `n>=2`, `m>=0`, and **each actually present named vertex** of `S(95^n,1^m)`—hub, one of `n` named long-arm vertices at any physical depth `1..95`, or one of `m` named short leaves—there **exists a graceful labeling depending on that selected vertex** which sends it to zero. At `m=0` no short leaf is quantified. The theorem is a full fixed-length zero-rotatability statement; one hub-zero labeling, ordinary gracefulness, one chosen arm, a bounded number of arms, and a sparse set of `m` are weaker statements.

For the accepted path transfer, a sufficient source at an interior depth `d` is **one and the same** graceful alpha labeling of `P191` with cut94, midpoint index95 labeled94, and an extreme `0` or190 at index `95-d` (or its reversed counterpart). The inherited alpha amalgamation then supplies arbitrary `n,m` and named arms; whole-graph complement handles a maximum source. A theorem that separately supplies the midpoint label and an interior extreme does not automatically supply this joint source. Nor is this particular source interface necessary for every possible direct spider proof.

## Path operations and recent spider sources

[Cattell, *Graceful labellings of paths* (2007), DOI 10.1016/j.disc.2007.03.046](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2007.03.046) is the material unresolved comparison. The [publisher abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0012365X07001215) promises broad one-vertex/one-label graceful path results and an alpha characterization using pi-representations. The publisher article/PDF returned HTTP403 in the fresh web read; the prior lawful access packet `graceful-cattell-p143-p191-access-2026-10-10-a/report.md` records additional 403/406/401 metadata routes and bounded author-copy searches without a readable original. Its original theorem numbers and transformations have **not** been verified here. The accessible 2017 Lemma5 and [Shan–Zhong (2026), Lemma2](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.14295v2) restate single prescribed-label path conclusions. They do not establish whether Cattell's *unread original proof* can impose the required midpoint, alpha cut and second extreme simultaneously. An unavailable proof might subsume the source family; no inference from access failure is valid.

[Adamaszek, *Efficient enumeration of graceful permutations* (2006)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0608513), PDF pp.1,4–5, proves an exponential count lower bound, defines endpoint-conditioned counts `G(r;a,b)`, and gives a prefix/endpoint concatenation in Lemma1. Its printed theorem does not prescribe an internal zero together with the P191 midpoint and alpha cut, or an all-vertex spider labeling. [Hicks–Ollis–Schmitt (2018)](https://community.middlebury.edu/~jschmitt/papers/HicksOllisSchmitt2018.pdf), PDF pp.14–15 Lemma4.5/proof, gives graceful-permutation constructions including balanced concatenation and a characterization of possible first edge differences. [Ollis (2020)](https://ajc.maths.uq.edu.au/pdf/78/ajc_v78_p035.pdf), PDF pp.19–20 Lemma5.5/Theorem5.6, gives bipartite endpoint freedom and a balanced insertion. These are substantial operation precursors. The inspected printed results do not *by themselves* assert the full k95 named-spider quantifiers; unclassified compositions may still yield every needed joint path word. The project has independently identified an HOS-style identity for its k23-to95 H1 branch, so that operation is not claimed new.

[Shan–Zhong, *Graceful Labeling of Two Families of Spiders* (2026), v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.14295v2), Theorems2–5, gives path attachment with label shift, ordinary gracefulness under rapidly increasing successive leg lengths, hub-zero with one arbitrary long leg and the rest length at most2, and ordinary gracefulness with at most three legs longer than2. Repeated 95-edge arms fail the length-growth condition, while its other profile bounds do not cover arbitrary `n` repeated 95-arms; no theorem here prescribes every vertex zero. Its Lemma1 restates the established alpha amalgamation.

[Dumitru–Nacu, *Alternating Extremes in Graceful Labelings of Full Binary Trees and Spider Trees* (2026), v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.12597), PDF pp.8–9 Theorem3.3/Corollary3.6, gives self-matched arm packing and, for each **fixed arm profile**, a graceful hub-one labeling after **sufficiently many** added hub leaves. The printed closure template includes one source zero, and thus gives real large-`m` ordinary-labeling overlap; it does not state all `m`, all target vertices, or a common threshold uniform in `n`. A separate project deduction using its packing theorem reaches some sparse large-`m` selected depths, but is **not** treated as a published full theorem. [Niu, *Graceful labelings of spiders with three-edge legs and pendant leaves* (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02303) is explicitly withdrawn in v2 on novelty grounds: the asserted ordinary result was already Bahls–Lake–Wertheim plus a leaf extension. It concerns length3 and underscores the need for a precise attribution check, not k95 subsumption.
